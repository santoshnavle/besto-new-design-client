Palghar Manufacturing & ZLD Facility

State-of-the-art closed-loop Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) ensuring 100% water recycling and zero environmental discharge.

PALGHAR PLANT ARCHITECTURE

Heavy-Duty Infrastructure & Automation

Located strategically in Dewan & Sons Udyog Nagar, Palghar East, our integrated manufacturing and reconditioning campus is engineered to process up to 1,500 steel drums and composite IBC units per shift with minimal manual intervention.

Combining hydraulic chimb reshaping, internal high-pressure caustic rotary jet washing, high-speed chain de-scaling, and computer-controlled lacquer baking tunnels, the facility restores industrial packaging to pristine structural integrity.

STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

The BESTOSIA® 4-Stage Reconditioning Line

Transforming hazardous-service industrial barrels into pristine, factory-calibrated containers with zero contamination.

01
Caustic Wash & De-scale

Drums undergo high-temperature caustic chemical scrubbing with dual rotary nozzles to completely dissolve oil residues, paints, and chemicals.

02
Hydraulic De-Denting

Automated hydraulic rollers expand internal shells, restoring true cylindrical geometry and precision chime seam alignment.

03
Pneumatic Submersion

Each barrel is pressurized to 0.3 bar and completely submerged under water in illuminated test tanks to confirm absolute leak-free integrity.

04
Baking & Painting

Interior epoxy coating is baked at 220°C in tunnel ovens, followed by electrostatic corporate exterior enamel painting and bung seals.

ZERO LIQUID DISCHARGE

Closed-Loop Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP)

In full compliance with MPCB environmental consent guidelines, our Palghar works operates an advanced on-site physical, chemical, and biological effluent treatment facility.

Industrial wash water containing hydrocarbons, resins, and surfactants is channeled through multi-stage primary coagulation, secondary clarifiers, dual-media filtration, and multi-effect evaporation. Over 98% of treated water is recycled back into barrel cleaning cycles.

Zero Outflow
Zero industrial effluent released outside boundary walls.
Sludge Disposal
Inert hazardous solid cake routed to authorized CHWTSDF facilities.
