Bestosia CMP — Close Mouth Plain Barrel

Bestosia CMP (Close Mouth Plain Barrel) is an industrial steel drum designed for the storage and transportation of liquids and other compatible industrial materials. It is generally manufactured from Mild Steel (MS) or CRCA steel sheets, with a plain, uncoated internal surface.

The barrel has a fixed top cover with filling and discharge openings (bung holes), making it suitable for controlled filling and emptying.

Nominal Capacity
200–210 Litres (Typical: 210L)
Material & Top Design

Main Components

  • Filling BungOpening for liquid filling.
  • Discharge / Vent BungSecondary opening for venting or discharge, depending on design.

Manufacturing Process

  1. Selection of approved-quality MS/CRCA steel sheets.
  2. Cutting and rolling of steel sheets.
  3. Formation of cylindrical barrel body.
  4. Longitudinal welding of the body.
  5. Manufacturing and fitting of top and bottom heads.
  6. Formation of bung openings.
  7. Assembly and seaming of top and bottom.
  8. External surface cleaning and painting.
  9. Fitting of bung plugs and sealing arrangements.
  10. Final dimensional and leakage inspection.

Industrial Applications

  • Industrial oils and lubricants.
  • Petroleum-based products, subject to compatibility.
  • Paints and industrial liquids.
  • Chemicals compatible with plain steel.
  • Industrial raw materials.
  • Liquid fertilizers.
  • General-purpose liquid storage and transportation.
Important: Since CMP barrels have an uncoated internal steel surface, they may not be suitable for corrosive chemicals or products requiring a protective internal lining. Verify chemical compatibility before use.

Quality Control Requirements

  • Proper steel sheet thickness.
  • Uniform welding and seaming.
  • Properly fitted bung plugs.
  • Leak-proof body and joints.
  • No dents, cracks or sharp edges.
  • Uniform external painting.
  • Dimensional inspection.
  • Leakage testing as per applicable standards.
  • IS 1783 compliance where applicable.
