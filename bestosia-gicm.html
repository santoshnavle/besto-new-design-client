Bestosia GICM — Galvanized Iron Close Mouth Barrel

Bestosia GICM (Galvanized Iron Close Mouth Barrel) is an industrial storage and transportation drum manufactured from steel sheets with a protective zinc coating. It features a fixed closed top with filling and secondary bung openings.

The galvanized coating helps protect the steel body against corrosion and rust, making it suitable for various industrial storage applications.

Main Components

  • Fixed Top CoverPermanently closed top construction.
  • Main Filling BungOpening for filling and handling liquids.
  • Secondary BungUsed for venting or controlled discharge, depending on design.

Manufacturing Process

  1. Selection of quality steel sheets.
  2. Application or procurement of zinc-coated steel sheets.
  3. Cutting and rolling into cylindrical shape.
  4. Formation and welding of the barrel body.
  5. Fabrication of top and bottom heads.
  6. Formation of bung openings.
  7. Assembly and seaming of top and bottom sections.
  8. Inspection of galvanized coating and welded areas.
  9. Fitting of bung plugs and sealing gaskets.
  10. Final dimensional and leakage inspection.
Quality Consideration: If the barrel is manufactured from pre-galvanized sheets, welding can damage the zinc coating around weld areas; verify how corrosion protection is maintained at these locations.

Advantages of GICM Barrels

  • Improved corrosion resistance compared with uncoated mild steel.
  • Durable metallic construction.
  • Suitable for industrial handling.
  • Fixed-top design reduces exposure to external contaminants.
  • Convenient filling through bung openings.
  • Reusable when properly inspected and maintained.
  • Suitable for various industrial packaging requirements.

Industrial Applications

  • Industrial liquid storage.
  • Lubricants and compatible oils.
  • Chemical raw materials.
  • Industrial process liquids.
  • Agricultural liquid products.
  • General-purpose material storage and transportation.
Certain acidic, alkaline and other chemical products can react with zinc. Always confirm chemical compatibility before use.
