HDPE Closed Top / Tight Head Drum

Bestosia HDPEC — HDPE Close Mouth Barrel

Bestosia HDPEC (HDPE Close Mouth Barrel) is an industrial plastic drum manufactured from High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE), designed for the storage, packaging and transportation of compatible liquids and industrial materials.

It features a closed-head design with a permanently moulded top and bung openings for filling, venting and controlled discharge.

  • Sealing GasketHelps prevent leakage at the closures.

Manufacturing Process

  1. Selection of approved HM-HDPE raw material.
  2. Preparation and melting of polymer granules.
  3. Extrusion of molten plastic parison.
  4. Moulding into the required drum shape.
  5. Cooling and solidification.
  6. Trimming and finishing of moulded edges.
  7. Formation and inspection of bung openings.
  8. Fitting of screw caps and sealing gaskets.
  9. Dimensional and weight inspection.
  10. Leakage and performance testing.

Advantages of HDPE Close-Mouth Barrels

  • Lightweight compared with conventional steel barrels.
  • Good resistance to many chemicals.
  • Corrosion-free plastic construction.
  • Good impact resistance.
  • Easy handling and transportation.
  • No internal rust formation.
  • Reusable where permitted and properly inspected.
  • Recyclable material.
  • Suitable for industrial liquid packaging.

Industrial Applications

  • Industrial chemicals and chemical raw materials.
  • Lubricants and compatible oils.
  • Agrochemicals and pesticides.
  • Pharmaceutical raw materials, subject to approval.
  • Detergents and liquid cleaning products.
  • Dyes, pigments and specialty chemicals.
  • Petrochemical products.
  • Industrial liquid storage and transportation.
