About Besto Container Industries

Over 28 years of engineering precision, statutory MPCB compliance, and circular economy leadership under the registered brand BESTOSIA®.

FOUNDED 1995

A Heritage Built on Containment Integrity

In 1995, Sayeed Ahmed established Besto Container Industries with a visionary objective: to provide Indian chemical and pharmaceutical manufacturers with safe, highly dependable industrial containment while establishing responsible packaging recycling practices.

Over nearly three decades, what began as a modest drum trading entity evolved into an integrated reconditioning and virgin manufacturing powerhouse. Today, operating under our proprietary trademarked brand BESTOSIA®, we process thousands of heavy-duty M.S. Barrels, Galvanized Iron Drums, and 1,000L Composite IBC Tanks monthly.

Our state-of-the-art campus in Palghar East is an operational model of sustainability, featuring a full-scale Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) Effluent Treatment Plant authorized by the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB).
