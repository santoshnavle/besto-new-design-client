Brand-New Mild Steel (M.S.) Barrels — Open Top & Close Mouth

Brand-New Mild Steel (M.S.) Barrels are heavy-duty cylindrical steel containers manufactured using quality mild steel sheets. They are designed for industrial storage, packaging and transportation of liquids, semi-liquids, chemicals, oils, grease, powders and other suitable materials.

Available in two main varieties: Open Top M.S. Barrels (Open Mouth) with removable top cover, locking ring and sealing gasket, and Close Mouth M.S. Barrels (Closed Top / Tight Head) with permanently fixed top cover and 2-inch & ¾-inch bung openings.

Standard Capacity & Gauge
200–210 Litres • 0.8–1.2 mm Sheet
Finish & Standards
Painted / Epoxy • IS 1783 / IS 5682 Ref.

Manufacturing & Quality Standards

  • Mild steel sheets of approved quality.
  • Uniform sheet thickness and proper welding.
  • Strong double/triple-seamed top and bottom joints, as applicable.
  • Proper leak testing for liquid packaging.
  • Good-quality external protective paint.
  • Internal epoxy coating wherever required.
  • Proper dimensional and weight inspection.
  • UN performance certification where required for transportation of regulated dangerous goods.

Relevant Indian standards include IS 1783 for steel drums and IS 5682 for open-top drums.

Advantages & General Requirements
