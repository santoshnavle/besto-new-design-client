Bestosia OTP — Open Top Plain Barrel

Bestosia OTP (Open Top Plain Barrel) is an open-mouth industrial barrel with a plain, uncoated internal surface. It is generally made from mild steel (MS) or CRCA steel sheets and is provided with a removable lid and locking arrangement.

Designed with a fully removable cover and reinforced rolling hoops for convenient handling of grease, pastes, powders, semi-solid materials, and plain-steel-compatible industrial products.

Common Capacity
210 Litres (Other capacities available)

Main Components

Applications

  • Storage of grease and lubricants.
  • Semi-solid industrial materials.
  • Resins and polymer products.
  • Industrial pastes and powders.
  • Chemical raw materials compatible with plain steel.
  • General industrial packaging.
Important: Plain internal steel has no protective epoxy lining. It may corrode or react with certain products. Confirm material compatibility before filling.
