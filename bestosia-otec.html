Bestosia OTEC — Open Top Epoxy Coated Barrel

Bestosia OTEC (Open Top Epoxy Coated Barrel) is an industrial packaging barrel generally manufactured from Mild Steel (MS) or CRCA steel sheets, with an epoxy coating applied to the internal surface to provide additional corrosion resistance and protect the stored material from direct contact with bare steel.

It features a full-opening removable lid, sealing gasket and locking ring arrangement for convenient filling, emptying and storage.

Typical Capacity
200–210 Litres (Standard: 210L)
Internal Lining
Epoxy / Phenolic Epoxy Lacquer (15–20 µm)

Manufacturing Process

  1. Selection of good-quality MS/CRCA steel sheets.
  2. Cutting and forming of cylindrical barrel body.
  3. Welding and fabrication of the body.
  4. Fitting of top and bottom components.
  5. Internal surface cleaning and preparation.
  6. Application of epoxy / phenolic lacquer coating.
  7. Oven baking or curing as per coating manufacturer's specifications.
  8. External painting and finishing.
  9. Fitting of removable lid, gasket and locking ring.
  10. Final inspection and quality testing.

Industrial Applications

  • Chemical and industrial raw material storage.
  • Pharmaceutical products, subject to coating approval.
  • Food ingredients, with appropriate food-contact certification.
  • Paints, resins and adhesives.
  • Agricultural and pesticide products.
  • Lubricants and grease.
  • Aromatic and specialty chemical products.

Difference Between OTP and OTEC Barrels

OTP – Open Top Plain
  • Bare steel internal surface.
  • No epoxy lining.
  • Generally lower purchase cost.
  • Suitable for compatible materials.
OTEC – Open Top Epoxy Coated
  • Epoxy-coated internal surface.
  • Better corrosion protection.
  • Additional coating process.
  • Suitable for compatible materials requiring protective lining.
