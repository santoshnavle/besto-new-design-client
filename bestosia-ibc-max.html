Bestosia IBC MAX — Export Optimized

An Intermediate Bulk Container (IBC) designed for bulk storage and transportation of liquids and industrial materials in domestic and export applications.

Besto Container Industries supplies reconditioned and thoroughly cleaned IBCs featuring an HDPE inner bottle, reinforced steel cage, steel or alternate pallet configurations, multiple valve options, and options from established brands such as Schutz and Kodama.

Export Optimization Features

  • Space Utilization: Cubic design helps optimize warehouse and container space.
  • Stackability: Depends on the manufacturer's tested stacking-load rating.
  • Leak Prevention: Properly tested outlet valves, seals and closures.
  • International Compliance: Appropriate UN certification for the intended dangerous-goods classification, where required.
  • Cargo Compatibility: Inner HDPE bottle and seals must be compatible with the exported product.
  • Traceability: Identification plate, manufacturing or reconditioning records and inspection documentation.

Export Documentation Checklist

Available documentation to confirm for export orders of Bestosia IBC MAX:

  1. UN certification and approval details, if applicable.
  2. Technical datasheet.
  3. Inner bottle material specification.
  4. Maximum gross weight and stacking test load.
  5. Leakage and pressure-test reports, where applicable.
  6. Cleaning and previous-cargo declaration.
  7. Export suitability confirmation.
