Brand-New 1000-Liter Composite IBCs

Brand-New 1000-Liter Composite IBCs (Intermediate Bulk Containers) are heavy-duty industrial containers designed for the storage and transportation of bulk liquids and other compatible materials. They generally consist of an inner High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) tank, an external galvanized steel protective cage and a supporting pallet.

They are widely used in chemical, pharmaceutical, food-processing, agricultural, petroleum and manufacturing industries.

Main Components

  • HDPE Inner TankStores the liquid and provides chemical resistance according to compatibility.
  • Galvanized Steel CageProtects and supports the inner container.
  • Pallet BaseSupports forklift and pallet truck handling.
  • Discharge ValveControls liquid discharge from the bottom outlet.

Available Types of 1000-Litre IBCs

  • Standard composite IBC with galvanized steel cage and plastic pallet.
  • Composite IBC with steel pallet.
  • Composite IBC with composite pallet.
  • Food-grade IBC with suitable food-contact certification.
  • UN-approved IBC for specified dangerous goods.
  • UV-protected IBC for outdoor storage or light-sensitive products.

Advantages & Applications

Key Advantages
  • Large storage capacity of 1000 litres.
  • Strong protective outer cage.
  • Convenient forklift handling.
  • Space-efficient rectangular construction.
  • Reusable, subject to inspection and service condition.
  • Easy filling and discharge.
  • Lower handling requirements compared with multiple smaller drums.
Applications
  • Industrial chemicals and liquid raw materials.
  • Lubricants and compatible oils.
  • Liquid fertilizers and agricultural products.
  • Water and industrial process liquids.
  • Food ingredients, where food-grade certification is available.
  • Pharmaceutical and manufacturing applications.

Quality & Safety Procurement Requirements

  1. Brand-new, unused IBC, not reconditioned.
  2. Virgin HDPE inner tank, if required.
  3. Galvanized steel protective cage.
  4. Strong, four-way forklift-entry pallet.
  5. Leak-proof filling cap and discharge valve.
  6. Suitable gasket and valve material for the intended liquid.
  7. UN certification and markings where dangerous-goods transport requires them.
  8. Manufacturer's technical data sheet and inspection documentation.
UN certification should be verified against the exact IBC design, contents, packing group and transport requirements. Food-grade suitability should also be documented separately.
