CIRCULAR ECONOMY & ESG COMPLIANCE

Palghar Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) Facility

Besto Container Industries takes statutory and environmental responsibility to the highest standards. Our operating works at Dewan & Sons Udyog Nagar, Palghar East features a dedicated closed-loop chemical Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP).

100% of drum washing wastewater is treated through multi-stage neutralization, mechanical filtration, and reverse osmosis, returning pure recycled water directly back to industrial washing cycles. Zero effluent is released into the biosphere.
