Brand-New Galvanized Iron (G.I.) Barrels

Brand-New Galvanized Iron (G.I.) Barrels are metal storage drums manufactured from steel sheets coated with zinc to provide corrosion resistance and durability.

They are suitable for industrial storage, transportation and general-purpose material handling across multiple capacity and top-closure configurations.

Available Capacities
50, 100, 210 and 235 Litres

Types of G.I. Barrels

  • Closed-Top BarrelWith sealed top and filling openings.
  • Open-Top BarrelWith removable lid and locking ring.

Applications

  • Industrial and commercial storage.
  • Chemical and solvent packaging, subject to compatibility.
  • Storage of oils and other industrial materials.
  • Transportation of suitable liquid and solid materials.
  • General-purpose material storage.
Important: For drinking water, food products or hazardous chemicals, verify internal coating, material compatibility and applicable safety certifications. Not every galvanized barrel is suitable for every substance.
